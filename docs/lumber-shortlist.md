## Summary — best bets for structural vs cladding

For a **climbable ~40 ft burnable** piece, prioritize **new untreated Douglas fir framing** (predictable grades, long lengths, posts up to 8×8 / 20') from East Bay full-service yards, and **reclaimed old-growth Douglas fir / redwood timbers and dimensional stock** from salvage yards that let you inspect for soundness and treatment. **Building Resources (SF)** and **The ReUse People / Urban Ore (East Bay)** look like the strongest **budget volume** plays for reclaimed 2× framing; **Heritage Salvage (Petaluma)** and **The Lumber Baron (Richmond)** are the best nearby yards for **heavy beams / timbers**. Use salvage boards, barnwood, and urban slabs mainly for **cladding / skin / interactive surface**, not primary climbable structure, unless an engineer grades specific members. **Avoid pressure-treated, CCA, creosote, railway ties, painted/lead-suspect stock, PVC, and glue-heavy engineered products (LVL/PSL/glulam)** for anything that will burn — Burning Man guidance explicitly lists treated lumber, glues, plastics, and many paints among materials that should not be burned.

## Cross-cutting burn & materials safety (Spilt Milk)

| Avoid for burnable structure | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| Pressure-treated (CCA, ACQ, CA, “ground contact”) | Untreated solid-sawn Douglas fir |
| Creosote / railway ties / marine piling | Untreated redwood / cedar cladding |
| Lead or oil-based painted lumber | Bare, scraped, or unfinished wood |
| PVC, plastics, rubber, asphalt, tar paper | Mechanical fasteners over adhesive where possible |
| Glue-laminated / LVL / PSL / heavy plywood-glue stacks | Solid timber & dimensional lumber |
| Particle board / MDF | Reclaimed solid boards |

Burning Man’s own “Burning Stuff” guidance: there are no fully “safe” burns, but **treated lumber, glues, plastics, paints (esp. lead/oil), and PVC are explicitly on the do-not-burn list**. Design the climbable structure so primary members are solid untreated wood; keep finishes minimal or water-based only on non-burn sacrificial layers if any.

**Structural caution:** Reclaimed lumber for a **climbable 40-ft** art piece should be **selected with a structural engineer / experienced BM build lead** — old-growth density helps, but insect damage, checks, nail holes, and unknown prior loading matter. Plan **new graded DF for critical load paths** + salvage for volume cladding/secondary members to balance budget (~$80k total project).
