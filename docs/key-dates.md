# Key dates

Snapshot of the shared Google Calendar named **Spilled Milk**. That calendar is the
live source. This page is a copy for the repo, not a second calendar.

**Last synced:** October 8, 2026 (Pacific).

All times are Pacific (`America/Los_Angeles`): **PT**, and **PDT** or **PST** when the
calendar entry says so.

**Approx** means the date was estimated from last year's pattern. Confirm those on
Burner Profiles before treating them as real. A working target is our planning date,
not a deadline copied off a funder's page.

Calendar titles use a color convention:

- Green: `Mutant Vehicle | ...`
- Blue: `Spilled Milk | Honoraria | ...`, `Spilled Milk | Art Grant | ...`, or
  `Spilled Milk | Registration | ...`

Tracks below match those titles: Honoraria, Art Grant, Registration, Mutant Vehicle,
and Event. The Event row (Burning Man itself) has no color in the convention above.

The table is chronological. The list under it is the same dates grouped by track.
Two Art Grant rows (the skipped Zellerbach cycle, and the CCI notification note) come
from calendar notes, not from their own holds.
