# Key dates

Snapshot of the shared Google Calendar named **Spilt Milk**. That calendar is the
live source. This page is a copy for the repo, not a second calendar.

**Calendar last copied:** October 8, 2026 (Pacific).

**Last verified against official pages:** October 19, 2026 (Pacific). Where a
funder's page and the October 8 calendar disagree, the row below follows the
funder's page and says so.

All times are Pacific (`America/Los_Angeles`): **PT**, and **PDT** or **PST** when
the source says so.

**Approx** means no exact 2027 day is posted. Confirm those on Burner Profiles
before treating them as real. A working target is the cycle this project is
aiming at. **Passed** means the deadline was before October 19, 2026. This repo
does not record a submission unless a status says one was sent.

Calendar titles use a color convention:

- Green: `Mutant Vehicle | ...`
- Blue: `Spilt Milk | Honoraria | ...`, `Spilt Milk | Art Grant | ...`, or
  `Spilt Milk | Registration | ...`

Tracks below match those titles: Honoraria, Art Grant, Registration, Mutant Vehicle,
and Event. The Event row (Burning Man itself) has no color in the convention above.

The table is chronological. The list under it is the same dates grouped by track.

## By track

### Honoraria

Q&A sessions on the LOI page: Mon Oct 5 (passed), Wed Oct 14 (passed), and Thu
Oct 22, 10:00–11:30am PDT. The BRC 2027 events list shows that Oct 22 session at
12:00 PM with no time zone. Check the RSVP confirmation before treating either
clock time as final.

Letter of intent due Thu Oct 29, 2026, 5:00pm PDT, via Burner Profiles on a
computer. Notifications Tue Dec 8, 2026. Full proposal Mon Jan 11, 2027, 5:00pm
PST, invite only. Award notifications early March 2027 on the LOI timeline; the
what-to-expect page says late February / early March.

Burning Man typically funds 40–60% of the total budget and says grants over
$20,000 are rare. The FAQ says to request partial funding. See `funding.md`.

before the end of 2026.
