# Open questions

## For the design

- **Stair width.** 4.5 ft passes every check. Wider needs a change elsewhere (deeper carton,
  raised entry). Which way do you want to go?
- **Slide speed.** The steepest section is about 67 degrees and riders will be moving fast
  at the puddle. Options: a short flat lip at the mouth, an earlier ease-out, a longer
  run-out, or a wider, higher-friction surface. Which feels right for a spill look?
- **Entry.** A door in the front face at grade is assumed. Is there somewhere else it
  should be, and does it need to be accessible?
- **Spout is centered.** The ridge runs across the middle of the carton's depth, so the launch
  deck needs a short bridge to the middle. The roof is also lower at the edges, so check
  headroom there.
- **Wood slide.** A painted-wood slide needs a surface that is smooth, durable in dust and
  wind, and safe for sliding.

## For an engineer and BRC art safety

- Structure of a 40 ft tipped wood shell, and the footing for a carton leaning on one corner.
- Support for a slide 20 ft in the air.
- Stairs: handrail height, baluster spacing, landing sizes, and loads for a crowd.
- Falls: guards on landings, and a way to keep people from crawling under them.
- Wind loading, which matters a lot on the playa.
- Fire plan for the burn, including what is inside and how it comes down.
- Whether a 40 degree stair-like surface or a 67 degree slide section is acceptable.

## For the numbers

- Check the carton proportions against a real half-gallon carton.
- Check the 2.8 ft sink against the footing design.
