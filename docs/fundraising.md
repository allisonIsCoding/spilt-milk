## Start here

1. **Point Reyes Farmstead** — private events form, plus a donation desk for cheese and experiences.
2. **Marin French Cheese** — the ranch already runs a public event series and private gatherings.
3. **Straus Family Creamery** — strongest formal product or cash channel, and the closest literal milk brand. Pair with a separate host if a party needs a room.

## Deprioritized on this pass

- Redwood Hill Farm / Capracopia (Sebastopol): spring tours only, and a weaker current sponsorship signal.
- Grocery private-label and national co-ops without a local event arm (Organic Valley as a national, unless a California community desk shows up later).
- Double 8 Dairy: reported closed, or production shifted.
