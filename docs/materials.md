# Materials sourcing

Where to look for reclaimed and cheap sturdy wood for a ~40 ft burnable,
climbable structure. Researched Oct 6, 2026 (PT) from public listings.
**Nothing has been ordered.** Prices, hours, phones, and per-yard notes are in
`lumber-shortlist.md`. Those prices are examples from that pass, not standing quotes.

The project total is still the working ~$80,000 in `budget.md`. Lumber is not
a priced line yet.

## What to buy for what

- **Critical load path:** new untreated Douglas fir, graded, from a full-service yard. Predictable, long lengths. Larsen lists posts through 8×8 and lengths commonly to 20 ft; confirm on a quote.
- **Volume reclaimed 2× framing:** Building Resources (San Francisco) and The ReUse People / Urban Ore (East Bay), if each piece is sound, dry, and untreated.
- **Heavy beams and timbers:** Heritage Salvage (Petaluma) and The Lumber Baron (Richmond).
- **Cladding, skin, interactive surfaces:** salvage boards, barnwood, urban slabs. Not primary climbable structure unless an engineer grades that member.
- **Plan:** new graded fir on the critical path, salvage for cladding and secondary members.

## Do not burn

Burning Man's own burning guidance: there is no fully safe burn. Treated lumber,
glues, plastics, paints (especially lead and oil), and PVC are on the do-not-burn
list. Design primary members as solid untreated wood.

| Avoid on anything that will burn | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Pressure-treated (CCA, ACQ, CA, "ground contact") | Untreated solid-sawn Douglas fir |
| Creosote, railway ties, marine piling | Untreated redwood or cedar cladding |
| Lead or oil-based painted lumber | Bare, scraped, or unfinished wood |
| PVC, plastics, rubber, asphalt, tar paper | Mechanical fasteners instead of adhesive where possible |
| Glue-laminated members, LVL, PSL, heavy glue-stacked plywood | Solid timber and dimensional lumber |
| Particle board, MDF | Reclaimed solid boards |

Reclaimed lumber in a climbable 40 ft piece needs a structural engineer or an
experienced Burning Man build lead. Old growth can be denser, and it can also
hide rot, checks, nail holes, and unknown prior loads. Fire plan and engineer
review are still open: `open-questions.md`.

## Yards

| Source | Where | Use for | Burn flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building Resources | Bayview, San Francisco | Reclaimed Douglas fir framing if sound and untreated | Inspect for pressure-treat tint, stamps, creosote, thick paint |
| Urban Ore, Building Materials Exchange | Berkeley | Secondary framing, bracing, cladding | Mixed paint, fasteners, occasional treated stock or glued plywood |
| The ReUse People | Oakland | Deconstruction framing and posts if inspected | They stock pressure-treated lumber. Refuse PT, ACQ, and CCA |
| The Lumber Baron | Richmond | New and FSC reclaimed fir and redwood; structure or cladding | Confirm "exterior" stock is not chemically treated |
| Heritage Salvage | Petaluma | Heavy timbers and beams | Check dock stock, paint, and creosote-era material |
| GreenLynx Reuse Store | Santa Rosa | Dimensional and old-growth, if inspected | Same salvage sort: treated, painted, glued panels |
| PlaceMakers | San Carlos | Old-growth structural or heavy cladding | Showroom skews heritage. Confirm volume. Prefer raw stock |
| Driftwood Salvage | East Palo Alto and demo sites, by appointment | Opportunistic framing from teardowns | Listings include pressure-treated posts. Skip those |
| Reusable Lumber Company | Pescadero, by appointment | Old-growth structural and cladding | Still check stamps and finishes |
| Economy Lumber | Oakland | New graded untreated fir; price benchmark against salvage | Do not buy their treated line or LVL / PSL for burn members |
| Larsen Lumber | San Leandro | Untreated fir or redwood posts and beams | Order the untreated line only, not the ACQ line |
| Urban Wood Rescue | Sacramento | Cladding and sculptural surfaces | Not a primary source for graded tower framing |
| Peroba Reclaimed Wood | Richmond | Cladding and feature surfaces | Buy unfinished |
| Ponderosa Millworks | Oakland | Aesthetic and cladding surfaces | Not bulk framing |
| CA Lumber Recycling | Carpinteria; claims Bay Area delivery | Backup if local yards cannot fill beams | Same treatment rules. Not a first visit |

Skip Ohmega Salvage (Berkeley): closed January 2023. East Bay Depot for Creative
Reuse is a craft-materials store, not a structural yard.

## Call order

1. Building Resources — walk the yard, price a sample of 2×6 / 2×8 / 2×10 and any beams, confirm untreated.
2. The ReUse People and Urban Ore — same-day East Bay loop for volume framing.
3. Heritage Salvage and The Lumber Baron — beam quotes. Ask about forklift loading.
4. Economy Lumber or Larsen — new untreated fir quote for the primary frame, as a ceiling if salvage runs short.
5. Driftwood, PlaceMakers, GreenLynx, Reusable Lumber — appointment or listing watch for long posts and old-growth packages.
6. At every yard: untreated fir or redwood in the size needed, whether treated stock is mixed into the pile, and delivery to an SF shop.

## Open before a serious quote

- Board-foot and linear-foot needs for primary structure vs skin. Not calculated yet.
- Whether the 2027 burn is the whole structure or a partial symbolic burn. That changes how strict the paint and salvage rules need to be. Related fire-plan question: `open-questions.md`.
- Crane, forklift, and flatbed access at the build site and on the way to Black Rock City.
- Engineer's minimum species and grade, and whether reclaimed wood may sit in the primary load path or only in cladding and secondary members.
